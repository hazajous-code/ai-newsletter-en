EDITOR'S NOTE

The longer the question, the more intent it reveals

Someone who typed "puppy food" into a search box asks AI, "My puppy is four months old — how many times a day should I feed him, and how much?" The longer the question, the more of the customer's circumstances end up in the sentence.

The problem is that there are fewer places to read those sentences. When AI gives the answer first, customers don't come to the website, and ad performance shows up only as numbers merged with regular search.

AI can quickly produce ad copy and product pages. Which question that copy should answer can only be decided by someone who has read the customer's long sentences directly.

When customers say three times as much, the side that reads the sentence to the end wins.

※ This is the editorial view of this issue, not a proven fact. The puppy food question is a hypothetical example.

01 · Key trend

AI Mode ads reach 10 countries — questions get longer, clicks get fewer

What changed

On Oct 28, alongside Alphabet's Q3 earnings, Google opened ads in AI Mode outside the US for the first time. Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore come first; Brazil, Mexico and the UK are added in Q4. In the US, Google started "multi-turn ads2," which also attach ads to answers to follow-up questions in a conversation. Korea is not on the announced list of countries.

Evidence

Pichai said growth in search queries was "the highest in the last five years" and that people's questions have become "longer, more personal and multimodal3." On the same earnings call, Chief Business Officer Philipp Schindler answered that "the strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we see in AI Mode." He added that he is "very positive about the current trajectory."

The numbers on the website side point the other way. In its Q3 shareholder letter, Reddit said search traffic "declined meaningfully and accelerated toward the end of the quarter." Logged-out daily users4 in the US stood at 27.3 million, down 4% from a year earlier. In Q4, Reddit will test a logged-in-only experience in some markets outside the US.

Marketing impact

Customers lay out their circumstances in full sentences in AI Mode. The information advertisers receive doesn't grow to match. Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max (PMax5) campaigns run in AI Mode automatically without any extra setup, and AI Mode can't be switched off or targeted separately. Performance is merged into regular search reports, and the questions that triggered ads also appear combined in the search terms report6.

The places where you can still read long questions are the search terms report and inquiries on your own channels. Keyword matching, where an ad runs only if those exact words are present, is used in AI Mode only when a query has "direct commercial intent." Broad match, AI Max7 and PMax are used in ways that also match ads to the context of the conversation.

Limits

"Three times longer" is Google's own figure, and the basis of comparison hasn't been disclosed. Google's statement that "sponsored and organic product listings have similar click-through rates" is based on a 15-day internal US measurement limited to product listings, and actual click-through rates weren't disclosed. Reddit also mentioned the possible drop in search rankings for machine-translated content as another factor in the traffic decline.

Verdict

Check now. Set a regular time each month to pull only the long questions from the search terms report and read them. While AI Mode performance can't be viewed separately, it is one of the few records that tell you what circumstances brought a customer to your ad.

02 · Policy change

One signal for "no AI training" — training and answer visibility are separate switches

What changed

On Oct 30, Google, Microsoft and OpenAI announced they would accept a website's choice to opt out of AI training through one common signal. The same day, Anthropic submitted a draft describing this signal to the IETF8, the international internet standards body. Until now, sites had to list each AI company's crawler name one by one in robots.txt9. Now a single group, User-Agent: __usage_ai-training, can send the same message to several companies.

Evidence

The draft is an individual draft without official IETF approval, and it expires on May 3, 2027. There is no way to penalize anyone who ignores the signal. Microsoft advised that "you can stop AI answers from using your content, but that may reduce your visibility in AI experiences."

Marketing impact

Product information and FAQs on a brand site have to be read by AI as answer sources for your name to come up in customers' questions. On Google, using the common signal just to block training also removes you from Gemini's answer sources. Paid or proprietary content and product information customers need to read shouldn't be handled with the same switch (editors' judgment).

Limits

It doesn't apply to material already collected. Meta, Amazon and Apple aren't on the list of participants. The draft's contents may change through IETF discussion.

Verdict

Decide in advance. Make a table that splits your site's content into "what customers should see in AI answers" and "what we don't want to hand over for training," and align robots.txt settings with your web team before the early-2027 rollout.

03 · This week

This week's news for marketers

  • Chrome fills in forms with Gmail content (from 10/28) "Enhanced autofill" finds things in Gmail such as order numbers, shipment tracking details and flight bookings, and suggests them for web forms (labeled "Gemini suggestion"). It applies only to personal accounts of users 18 and older who have turned on personalization in Gemini in Chrome, and launched first on desktop, Android and iOS in the US. The European Economic Area, Switzerland and the UK are excluded. AI is starting to fill in information customers used to type themselves on your order and booking screens.
  • Meta's AI agent12 Muse enters a fourth market, Singapore (10/27) After the US, Canada and Japan, it's the fourth market in seven weeks. It launched connected to Shopee, Grab, Singapore Airlines, Agoda, Lazada and others, with more apps to be added over time. It asks users for confirmation before sending email or making a purchase. If the agent chooses and buys on the customer's behalf inside connected apps, brands not on the connected list will struggle to make the customer's shortlist (editors' judgment).
  • Reddit tests a "log in to view" Reddit (Q4) Saying logged-in and app users account for more than 80% of monetization, Reddit will test a logged-in-only experience in some markets outside the US. It cited personalization, ad-targeting signals and data protection as reasons. With search traffic falling, it's a decision to focus on users with a direct relationship.

04 · Platform updates

Operational changes to check this week

Platform update alert

  • Google AI Mode ads: expanded to 10 countries (10/28); Brazil, Mexico and the UK in Q4. Korea is not on the announced list. Existing Search, Shopping and PMax campaigns become eligible automatically, and AI Mode can't be switched off on its own. Negative keyword13 and brand exclusion settings still apply.
  • Google multi-turn ads: launched in the US. They appear with a "Sponsored" label in answers to follow-up questions in a conversation.
  • Common AI training opt-out signal: applies from early 2027 for Google and Feb 1, 2027 for Microsoft. Microsoft opens interim grounding settings in Bing Webmaster Tools from Dec 15, and sites that have relied on the existing NoArchive/NoCache restrictions must apply the new settings by Mar 1, 2027 to keep those restrictions.
  • Chrome enhanced autofill: for US personal accounts (opt-in). If you run payment or booking forms for US customers, check that autofilled values are processed correctly.

GLOSSARY

Glossary

  1. AI Mode The conversational search view inside Google Search. It shows an AI-written answer first instead of a list of links, then takes follow-up questions. ↩
  2. Multi-turn ads Ads attached to the second, third and later questions in a conversation with AI. The whole flow of the conversation, not just the first search, becomes an ad opportunity. ↩
  3. Multimodal Asking and answering with photos, voice and documents, not just text — like snapping a photo of a broken part and asking, 'How do I fix this?' ↩
  4. Logged-out users Visitors who click through from search results without signing in, read, and leave. They are the first to drop when search traffic falls. ↩
  5. PMax (Performance Max) A Google campaign type where you set only the goal, and Google's AI places ads across Search, YouTube, Shopping and other placements on its own. ↩
  6. Search terms report A table in the ads dashboard showing which words customers actually searched when your ad appeared. ↩
  7. AI Max An AI feature added to Google search ads. It finds similar queries beyond your registered keywords to show ads, and adapts ad text and landing pages to the query. ↩
  8. IETF The international body that sets the shared rules (standards) the internet runs on — agreements that let different companies communicate in the same way. ↩
  9. robots.txt A notice file in which a website tells the automated collection programs (crawlers) of search engines and AI what they may and may not take. ↩
  10. Google-Extended A name tag Google uses in robots.txt. Blocking it keeps a site in Google Search but stops its content being used for Gemini training and as a source for Gemini answers. ↩
  11. Grounding When AI looks up and reads web pages to use as the basis for an answer. If training is 'studying in advance,' grounding is closer to 'checking sources while answering.' ↩
  12. AI agent Unlike a chatbot that only answers questions, an AI that, given a goal, breaks it into steps, uses apps and carries out the actual task. ↩
  13. Negative keyword An excluded term telling the system, 'Don't show my ad for searches containing this word.' It keeps ad spend from leaking to irrelevant questions. ↩
